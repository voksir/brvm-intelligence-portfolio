The 7 AI Agents — Heart of the System

Each agent was designed, configured, and connected to real BRVM data using the TradingAgents 0.2.x multi-agent framework. Together they form a complete investment research workflow — from raw market data to an actionable recommendation.

Why 7 agents on TradingAgents?

TradingAgents provides the LangGraph orchestration layer — I customized each agent's system prompts (French, FCFA, BRVM context) and wired a custom Supabase data vendor ('brvm'). Specialization + structured debate produces more balanced, auditable recommendations.

Full Agent Documentation →
Agent 1

Fundamentals Analyst 📊

Evaluates financial health of a BRVM stock
  • PER vs sector average
  • DNPA (dividend per share) in FCFA
  • Net yield & market compartment
  • Order book & real liquidity
Source: Official BRVM BOC (Bulletin Officiel de la Cote)
Agent 2

Market / Technical Analyst 📈

Reads price and volume signals
  • Daily OHLCV history
  • 30 / 60 / 90-day price trends
  • MACD, RSI, moving averages
  • Beta & relative volatility
Source: Sikafinance via BRVM data pipeline
Agent 3

News Analyst 📰

Interprets macro events impacting the stock
  • Company-related market news
  • UEMOA macro context: market PER, risk premium
  • Sector index performance (TEL, FIN, CB…)
  • Daily leaderboard: top gainers & losers
Source: Supabase news + boc_market_summary
Agent 4

Sentiment Analyst 📡

Captures market mood around the ticker
  • News sentiment trends
  • Relative performance vs market
  • Volume anomalies & unusual price gaps
Source: Aggregated from news & market data
Agents 5 & 6

Bull Researcher & Bear Researcher ⚖️

Adversarial debate from the 4 analyst reports
  • Bull: growth potential, undervaluation, positive indicators
  • Bear: risks, overvaluation, BRVM liquidity, macro headwinds
  • Structured debate reduces single-model bias
Output: investment_plan field in JSON
Agent 7

Trader + Risk Manager 🎯

Synthesizes debate into final recommendation
  • Final decision: BUY / HOLD / SELL
  • Confidence level
  • Detailed action plan with justification
  • BRVM-specific risk assessment (liquidity, daily fixing)
Output: final_trade_decision in PDF bulletin

Application Domains

BRVM Intelligence is designed for real-world use cases across the UEMOA financial ecosystem — from individual investors to institutional research and emerging FinTech platforms.

👤 Retail Investors

Receive a clear French-language analysis bulletin every evening after market close. Make informed BRVM decisions without being a financial analyst, and discover value opportunities identified by the agents.

🏦 Broker-Dealers (SGI)

Scale research capacity without hiring additional analysts. Produce structured research notes for institutional clients and automatically flag unusual market patterns across the watchlist.

🌍 Ivorian & UEMOA Diaspora

Invest in the BRVM from abroad with up-to-date analysis. Understand the UEMOA market context — FCFA, daily fixing, sector dynamics — without prior financial expertise.

🎓 Academic Research

Explore 15+ years of BRVM data (2010–2026) for theses and publications. Backtest investment strategies on one of Africa's most structured regional equity markets.

📱 FinTech & WealthTech Platforms

Embed automated nightly BRVM analysis into neobanks, robo-advisors, or mobile investment apps. Structured JSON output and PDF bulletins ready for API integration and client-facing dashboards.
