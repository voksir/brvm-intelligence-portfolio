Behind the Scenes — CLI Background Workflow

The PDF bulletin is the visible output — but before the final BUY / HOLD / SELL decision, the 7 AI agents run a full research cycle in the background. With python run_daily.py --ticker SNTS.sn --date 2026-03-28 --debug, TradingAgents streams every step to the terminal: pipeline initialization, data tool calls, structured agent reasoning, adversarial debate, and finally PDF + JSON generation. Below is that workflow captured live (SNTS.sn — Sonatel, 2026-03-28).

Step 1

Pipeline Initialization & Analysis Launch

Command: run_daily.py --ticker SNTS.sn --date 2026-03-28 TradingAgentsGraph initialized with vendor 'brvm'

What you see: BAQA starts, loads the BRVM config, initializes the LangGraph orchestration layer, and enters single-ticker analysis mode for SNTS.sn.
Why it matters: This is the entry point — one CLI command triggers the entire multi-agent pipeline end-to-end.

Step 2

Data Retrieval — get_stock_data Tool Call

Agent: Market / Technical Analyst Source: daily_quotes via brvm_supabase.py 22 trading days · FCFA · BRVM/UEMOA

What you see: The Market Analyst agent invokes get_stock_data and receives real OHLCV history — Open, High, Low, Close, Volume in shares and XOF, daily variation %.
Why it matters: Agents don't hallucinate prices — they reason on live Supabase data pulled through the custom 'brvm' vendor.

Step 3

Technical Indicators — get_indicators Tool Call

Agent: Market / Technical Analyst Indicators: ATR, close prices, volume, variation

What you see: A second tool call returns computed indicators (ATR, close, volume, daily variation) formatted for LLM consumption.
Why it matters: Demonstrates the tool-use pattern — agents call specialized data functions, then interpret the results in natural language.

Step 4

Agent Reasoning — Fundamentals Conclusion

Agent: Fundamentals Analyst Fields: PER, DNPA, yield, dividend history Output: FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL

What you see: The Fundamentals Analyst synthesizes BOC data into a structured conclusion — company profile table (PER 6.87, DNPA 1 655 FCFA, yield 5.83%) and an initial transaction proposal.
Why it matters: Each agent produces auditable, structured reasoning in French — not a black-box score. This feeds into the Bull vs Bear debate before the Trader's final call.

Step 5

Final Decision & PDF Generation

Agent 7: Trader + Risk Manager Decision: SELL (debug run) Outputs: BRVM_Bulletin_*.pdf + BRVM_*.json

What you see: After the full agent cycle (4 analysts → Bull/Bear debate → Trader), the pipeline logs the final decision (SELL in this debug run), then report_generator.py assembles the professional PDF bulletin and saves the raw JSON state.
Why it matters: The complete loop — from one CLI command to an actionable recommendation and a client-ready PDF, in under 3 minutes.

Agent 1 — Fundamentals Analyst

📊 Financial Health Evaluation

Role: Assess whether a BRVM stock is fundamentally sound for investment

This agent reads official BOC (Bulletin Officiel de la Cote) data and compares the stock against sector benchmarks.

  • PER (Price Earnings Ratio) — current vs sector average
  • DNPA — Net Dividend Per Share in FCFA
  • Net yield and market compartment (Prestige / Principal)
  • Order book depth and real trading liquidity
Data source: Supabase boc_daily, boc_order_book, stocks
JSON output: fundamentals_report
PDF section: Analyse Fondamentale
Agent 2 — Market / Technical Analyst

📈 Price & Volume Signal Analysis

Role: Detect trends, momentum, and technical entry/exit signals
  • Daily OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) history
  • Price trends over 30, 60, and 90 days
  • Technical indicators: MACD, RSI, moving averages (50 SMA, 200 SMA)
  • Bollinger Bands for reversal signals
  • Beta and volatility relative to the BRVM market
Data source: daily_quotes via Sikafinance pipeline
JSON output: market_report
PDF section: Analyse Technique & Marche
Agent 3 — News Analyst

📰 Macro & Company News Interpretation

Role: Connect external events to the stock's investment thesis
  • Recent market news related to the company
  • UEMOA macro context: market PER, risk premium, liquidity
  • Sector index performance (TEL, FIN, CB, IND, ENE…)
  • Daily palmares: strongest gainers and losers on BRVM
Data source: news, boc_market_summary, boc_sector_indices, palmares
JSON output: news_report
PDF section: Analyse des Actualites
Agent 4 — Sentiment Analyst

📡 Market Mood & Alert Signals

Role: Gauge investor sentiment and detect unusual market behavior
  • Sentiment trend from recent news flow
  • Stock performance vs BRVM composite (outperformance / underperformance)
  • Alert signals: volume anomalies, unusual price gaps
Data source: news + market comparison data
JSON output: sentiment_report
PDF section: Sentiment de Marche
Agents 5 & 6 — Bull & Bear Researchers

⚖️ Adversarial Investment Debate

Role: Force balanced analysis through structured contradiction

Both researchers receive the 4 analyst reports and must argue their case before the Trader decides.

  • Bull Researcher argues FOR investment: positive indicators, growth potential, undervaluation
  • Bear Researcher argues AGAINST: identified risks, overvaluation, low BRVM liquidity, unfavorable macro
  • Structured debate reduces single-model confirmation bias
Input: 4 analyst reports
JSON output: investment_plan
PDF section: Plan d'Investissement (Debat Bull vs Bear)
Agent 7 — Trader + Risk Manager

🎯 Final Recommendation & Risk Assessment

Role: Synthesize all analysis into an actionable investment decision
  • Final decision: BUY / HOLD / SELL
  • Confidence level of the recommendation
  • Detailed action plan with full justification
  • BRVM-specific risk factors: daily fixing (no intraday trading), liquidity constraints
Input: Bull vs Bear debate + all reports
JSON output: final_trade_decision
PDF output: Colored decision box + Decision Finale section
